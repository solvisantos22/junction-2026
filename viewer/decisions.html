    The fixed mission contract

    100 − 50 × 0.5 = 75

    Every rover that survives all 50 ticks pays the same 25 energy in idle drain. Staying alive at the horizon requires strictly less than 75 action energy.

    Waiting changes deadlines and opportunities; it does not reduce this fixed idle bill. Returning to base does not recharge the rover.

    This is a delayed-command simulation with explicit toy rules, not a faithful model of Mars rover operations.
