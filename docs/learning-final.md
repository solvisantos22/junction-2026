# Learning on the final frozen Traverse version

This is a new experiment on the team’s frozen normal-Traverse rules and return-home navigator, following Sölvi’s instruction to wait for the final map before larger training and the owner’s merged readiness reply. It is not MAX. The source base is main `496ba56`; exact source hashes and runner commit are published before launch. The earlier six-run pilot remains unchanged under its original freeze `e8a8acb`, including its paused continuation mechanism. Never rewrite historical hashes to make old results appear to use the final navigator.

## Why continue, and what changed

The old 12,288-mission pilot completed. At 2,048 missions per initialization, PPO validation averaged .376800 versus .087395 initially; replay averaged .364144. Their respective512→2048 gains were .032777 and .087550. All576 retained validation missions returned home alive, with zero invalid calls. These are reused validation results and evidence to continue studying both methods, not final generalization or reference superiority. The matched old-navigator controls were greedy .380555 and beam .514345.

Physics, map generation and planning files are unchanged. `Pilot._drive` now permits a smaller reserve and partial affordable moves when going home; the goal-selection adapter inherits that real behavior change. A new source freeze is required. This experiment starts all six learners from scratch on the final navigator, so no old replay-buffer transition or optimizer state is silently transferred.

PPO and the Rainbow-inspired Double-DQN/prioritized-replay/three-option-step learner retain the exact architecture and hyperparameters of the prior [protocol](learning-campaign.md). Neither is an LLM, and the replay method is not full Rainbow. The same public20-candidate/14-global feature interface,64-wide pooled candidate model, persistent goal options, charging-feasibility helper and original official normalized terminal reward apply. No hidden state is supplied to the actor; no reward shaping, teacher imitation or environment rule changes are introduced.

Freeze both source closures and all policy hashes before opening cases. For each learned method, a compatibility screen requires new-minus-old mean reward at least−.01, no deaths/invalid calls, and no more than one lost home outcome across its24 paired cases. A failed screen stops the larger launch pending diagnosis and a separate decision; no silent repair/retry.

Evaluate greedy and the final frozen beam reference on the same worlds. No tuning or checkpoint selection from final cases.

The primary mission comparison is learned-minus-initial; learned-versus-greedy/beam is also required. Keep the project’s frozen planner as its reference unless the team separately approves a change supported by comparable score and reliability. A learning gain alone proves neither reference superiority nor Claude failure.
