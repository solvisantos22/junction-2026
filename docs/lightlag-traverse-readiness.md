# Lightlag Traverse: answer to the team's readiness questions

An answer from the Traverse owner to [`docs/learning-campaign-results/team-training-readiness.md`](learning-campaign-results/team-training-readiness.md), in its reply template. It states the technical state of the code. The choice of the team's final project is not made here.

- **Owner/session and timestamp:** Kristján / Claude Code (gap research chat, worktree `claude-gap-research`), Saturday 14 November about 20:45 Helsinki.
- **Proposed final commit, environment and Harbor task path:** as Kristján froze it (`TEAM_STATUS.md`, commit `5af35db`, relayed by his other session; not yet on `origin/main` when this was written):
  - rules: the Lightlag Traverse 0.1.0 rules, unchanged;
  - reference: the pilot of commit `1545627` (`lightlag_traverse/pilot.py`: 0.1.0's pilot plus the way-home rule `home_floor`);
  - submission candidate: the three-mission campaign, `harbor/lightlag-traverse-campaign/` from commit `3bff7b2`, on worlds 2027, 2028 and 2029; fallback: the one-mission task `harbor/lightlag-traverse/` on world 2026.
  - These commits are on branch `worktree-claude-gap-research`, not on `main` yet; Kristján is merging it. It is normal Traverse and the campaign of it, not MAX and not a combined version.
- **Rules still changing:** none. The sunrise-to-sunset day was tried and dropped.
- **What changed in `lightlag_traverse/` compared with what is on `main` (the version RL studies pinned):** `environment.py`, `terrain.py`, `surface.py`, `planning.py`, `play.py`, `sandbox*.py` and `oneshot.py` are byte for byte unchanged, so the simulator, the map generation and the planner are the same. Changed: `pilot.py` (`home_floor`, `fly_campaign`), `server.py` (can serve a campaign), `make_task.py` (builds both tasks); new: `campaign.py`. A pin over every `lightlag_traverse/*.py` therefore changes, but nothing a single-mission training adapter reads behaves differently.
- **Integration notes, none blocking for a single-mission adapter:**
  - The way-home rule gives identical scores to the 0.1.0 pilot on development worlds 1 to 24, fresh worlds 1000 to 1011 and world 2026; it acts only when the battery is low on the way home (it does on campaign site 1).
  - In the campaign, every mission is an unchanged `TraverseEnvironment`. The next begins at once, at its tick 0, when the last tick of one passes. Packets, acknowledgements, `map()` and `advance` replies gain a `mission` field, and `advance` gains `campaign_complete`.
  - The campaign's score is the value credited in all missions over all the value there was, not the mean of the missions' scores; the team's `grade.py` reads it unchanged.
